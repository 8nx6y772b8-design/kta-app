KTA WorkOS

Privacy Policy

Kiwi Trade Apprentices (KTA) · Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KTA WorkOS ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information. By using KTA WorkOS, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

KTA WorkOS is operated by Kiwi Trade Apprentices, a New Zealand organisation supporting trades apprentices and their employers. You can contact us at admin@kta.org.nz.

2. What information we collect

We collect the following information when you use KTA WorkOS:

  • Name and email address (used for login and communications)
  • Timesheet entries (dates, hours worked, entry types, notes)
  • Leave requests (dates, leave type, status)
  • Employment details (role, employer, allocated contacts)
  • Compliance records (licence numbers, expiry dates, certifications)
  • Messages sent within the app
  • Device information for push notifications (if enabled)

3. How we use your information

Your information is used to:

  • Operate the timesheet and leave management system
  • Notify approvers and apprentices of timesheet and leave status changes
  • Generate payroll data for submission to Xero (where applicable)
  • Maintain training and compliance records
  • Send in-app and email notifications relevant to your role

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. How your data is stored

KTA WorkOS uses Supabase for data storage, a cloud database provider with servers hosted in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Access to your data is controlled by role-based permissions — you can only access records relevant to your role.

5. Who can see your data

  • Apprentices can see their own timesheet entries, leave requests, and messages
  • Approvers and Mentors can see records for apprentices allocated to them
  • KTA Administrators have access to all records for operational purposes

6. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for compliance and operational purposes. If you request deletion, we will remove your personal data within 30 days.

7. Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Raise a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner

8. Cookies and local storage

KTA WorkOS uses browser local storage to maintain your login session. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

9. Push notifications

If you enable push notifications, your device's push token is stored securely and used only to deliver app notifications. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact us

For privacy enquiries, data access requests, or deletion requests, please contact us at admin@kta.org.nz or visit crmkta.com.
