1. Logging In

  1. Open crmkta.com in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox).
  2. Enter your email address and password.
  3. Click Sign In →.
💡 Tip — Save to Home Screen

On your phone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or the install banner (Android) to open the app like a native application.

Forgot Your Password?

  1. On the login screen, tap Forgot your password?
  2. Enter your email address and tap Send Reset Link.
  3. Check your inbox for the reset email and follow the link to create a new password.

2. Your Home Screen

When you log in, you'll land on the Timesheet screen. This is your main workspace. At the top you'll see four summary cards:

CardWhat It Shows
Today (mine)Hours logged today across your apprentices
This Week (mine)Total hours this week
Visible EntriesTotal number of entries you can see
Pending ApprovalEntries waiting for your action

Below the summary cards, each of your apprentices appears in their own card, showing their name, hours breakdown, and any entries needing approval.

3. Approving Timesheets

When an apprentice submits their timesheet entries, a yellow "Pending Approval" banner appears on their card.

Approve a Full Week

  1. Find the apprentice's card on your home screen.
  2. In the yellow banner, you'll see a button like:
    ✓ Approve week ending 29/03/2026 (5 entries · 40h)
  3. Click the button.
  4. A confirmation dialog will ask: "Approve week ending 29/03/2026 for [Name]?"
  5. Click Confirm.
  6. Done! The apprentice receives an email confirming their entries are approved.
💡 Approve Multiple Weeks at Once

If an apprentice has entries from more than one week, an "Approve all pending" button appears. Click it to approve everything in one go.

Approve a Single Entry

You can also approve entries one at a time:

  1. Find the entry row in the apprentice's table.
  2. On the right-hand side, click the green ✓ button.
  3. The entry status changes to ✓ Approved.

4. Declining Timesheets

If an entry has incorrect hours or details, you can decline it so the apprentice can fix and resubmit.

  1. Find the entry row in the apprentice's table.
  2. Click the red ✕ button on the right.
  3. The entry is marked ✕ Declined.
  4. The apprentice receives an email notification asking them to correct and resubmit.
⚠️ Changed Your Mind?

If you declined an entry by mistake, you can click the ↺ (re-approve) button that appears next to declined entries.

5. Approving via Email

You don't always need to open the app. When an apprentice submits entries, you receive an email with one-click approve/decline buttons.

  1. Open the notification email from KTA.
  2. Review the entry details (date, hours, type, note).
  3. Click ✓ Approve or ✕ Decline directly in the email.
  4. You'll see a confirmation page. That's it — no login required.
⚠️ Email Links Expire

The approve/decline links in emails are valid for 7 days. After that, you'll need to approve through the app.

6. Leave Requests

When an apprentice requests leave, it follows a two-stage approval process:

📤 Apprentice Submits
→
✓ You Approve
→
★ KTA Final Approval

You are Stage 1. Once you approve, it goes to KTA for final sign-off.

Viewing Leave Requests

Leave requests appear in the Leave Requests panel on your Timesheet screen. Requests are organised into tabs:

TabMeaning
⏳ Pending ApproverWaiting for your decision
✓ Awaiting KTAYou approved — waiting for KTA final approval
★ Fully ApprovedBoth you and KTA have approved
✕ DeclinedRequest was declined

Approving a Leave Request

  1. Go to the ⏳ Pending Approver tab.
  2. Review the request card — it shows the apprentice's name, leave type (Annual, Sick, Bereavement, or Other), and the date range.
  3. Click the ✓ Approve button.
  4. The request moves to the ✓ Awaiting KTA tab.
  5. The apprentice receives an email confirming your approval.
💡 What Happens After You Approve?

Once KTA gives final approval, the system automatically creates 8-hour timesheet entries for each working day (Monday–Friday) within the leave dates. Public holidays are excluded.

Declining a Leave Request

  1. Click the ✕ Decline button on the leave request card.
  2. A form appears asking for a reason — this is required.
  3. Type your reason and click Confirm Decline.
  4. The apprentice receives an email with your reason.

7. Contacting KTA

Need to get in touch with the KTA team? Use the Contact Us section at the bottom of your Timesheet screen.

  1. Scroll down to the Contact Us card.
  2. Tap on a team member to select them (they'll have a teal checkmark ✓).
  3. Choose how to contact them:
    • 📱 Call — opens their phone number
    • ✉️ Email — opens their email address
    • 💬 Message in App — send an in-app message

Sending an In-App Message

  1. Select a team member and click 💬 Message in App.
  2. Type your message in the text box.
  3. Click Send Message.
  4. You'll see a green confirmation: "Message sent!"

9. FAQ & Troubleshooting

I can't see any apprentices on my screen?
You can only see apprentices who have been allocated to you. Contact KTA if you believe an apprentice is missing from your list.
An apprentice says they submitted but I don't see anything?
Ask the apprentice to check that they actually tapped Submit (not just saved as draft). You can also try refreshing the page.
I approved by mistake — can I undo it?
Once approved, the entry cannot be un-approved from the app. Contact KTA and they can update the entry status for you.
I declined by mistake — can I fix it?
Yes. A ↺ (re-approve) button appears next to declined entries. Click it to approve the entry.
The email approve/decline links aren't working?
Email links expire after 7 days. If expired, log into the app at crmkta.com and approve from there.
The page looks outdated or isn't loading properly?
Try a hard refresh: press Cmd + Shift + R (Mac) or Ctrl + Shift + R (Windows). This clears the cached version and loads the latest update.
I forgot my password?
On the login screen, tap Forgot your password?, enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox.
Can I approve timesheets from my phone?
Yes! The app works on all devices. You can also approve directly from notification emails without logging in.
What happens after I approve a leave request?
Your approval is Stage 1. The request is forwarded to KTA for final approval. Once KTA approves, timesheet entries are automatically created for the leave days.
